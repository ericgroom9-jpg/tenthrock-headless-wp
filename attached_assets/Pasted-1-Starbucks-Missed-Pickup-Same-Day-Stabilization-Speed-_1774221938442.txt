6. Compactor Failure - Emergency Site Stabilization (Project Management) 
A big box retail location experienced a compactor failure after the unit became overfilled, forcing the door open and preventing the hauler from safely transporting it under DOT regulations. With waste continuing to accumulate behind the building, the situation quickly became a sanitation and operational risk.
Tenth Rock deployed a stabilization crew to remove enough material to reduce the load and secure the compactor door, allowing the hauler to safely remove the unit for service. To maintain waste capacity at the site, Tenth Rock arranged for a same-day open-top dumpster delivery while coordinating vendors to clean and prepare the compactor area for repair upon its return. The site was stabilized quickly, allowing normal operations to continue with minimal disruption to daily workflow.
