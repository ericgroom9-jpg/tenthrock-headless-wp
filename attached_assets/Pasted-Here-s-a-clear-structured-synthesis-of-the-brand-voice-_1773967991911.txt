# **1. Brand Positioning**

### **Core Position**

A **fast, on-demand waste solutions partner** for businesses—built for urgent, complex, and large-scale situations where traditional providers fail.

### **Expanded Position (Future State)**

* Moving from **emergency-only vendor → strategic, go-to waste partner**
* From **subcontractor (via Rubicon) → direct enterprise relationships**
* From **transactional jobs → long-term contracts & recurring partnerships**

### **Differentiation**

* **Speed & responsiveness** → “We show up when no one else can”
* **Specialization** → Waste only (vs competitors doing everything)
* **Nationwide + local execution** → “Power of a network, precision of local teams”
* **Operational coordination** → Not just hauling, but managing complex, multi-step jobs
* **Cost efficiency at scale** → Better pricing via vendor network + fewer costly mistakes

### **Target Audiences**

Dual audience strategy:

1. **Primary (70%)**: C-suite / enterprise decision-makers
2. **Secondary (30%)**: Facilities managers / operators (initial touchpoint)

---

# **2. Brand Voice**

### **Tone Characteristics**

* **Corporate but not stiff**
* **Casual, direct, no fluff**
* **Confident, competent**
* **Problem-solving oriented**
* **Calm under pressure**

### **How it should feel**

* “We’ve got this handled”
* Not overly polished or salesy
* Not playful or gimmicky
* Clear, practical, and grounded

### **Voice Spectrum (from exercise)**

* Corporate > Playful
* Innovative > Traditional
* Accessible > Exclusive
* Balanced Calm + Confidence (reassuring, not hype)
* Casual > Formal
* Midpoint: Authoritative + Customizable
* Affordable but high-quality
* Creative (not boring), slightly bold

# **4. Strategic Messaging Challenge (Important Insight)**

A key tension to solve:

### **Selling Before the Problem Exists**

* Many prospects don’t need the service *yet*
* Need to position as:

  * **Preparedness partner**
  * **Backup plan**
  * **Go-to when things go wrong**

👉 Messaging should shift from:

* “We fix emergencies”
  → to:
* “You’ll want us in place before the emergency happens”
